Frequently Asked.

Do you sell the products on this site?

No. MyCampusKorner is a curated guide to college essentials. When you choose something, you buy it from the retailer on their own website, and they handle payment, shipping and returns.

I have a problem with an order. Who should I contact?

Please contact the retailer you bought from: they have your order details and handle shipping, returns and refunds. If something on our site is wrong, like a broken link or out-of-date information, let us know with the form above.

I represent a brand. How can we work together?

Choose “Partnership / affiliate” as the reason in the form above and tell us about your products. We are always looking for items that genuinely help students.
