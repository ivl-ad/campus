Frequently Asked.

How do I submit a product for review?

To submit a product for review, please visit our website and navigate to the 'Product Review' section. There, you will find a submission form that requires details about your product, including its name, description, and any relevant images. Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to facilitate a smooth review process.

Once submitted, our team will evaluate your product and get back to you within 5-7 business days. If you have any questions during the process, feel free to reach out to our support team.

Can I apply to be a student brand ambassador?

Yes, we welcome applications for our Student Brand Ambassador program! This program is designed for enthusiastic students who are passionate about our brand and want to promote it on their campus. To apply, please fill out the application form available on our website.

In your application, be sure to include your contact information, a brief description of why you want to be an ambassador, and any relevant experience you may have. We review applications on a rolling basis and will contact you if you are selected for an interview.

Do you offer bulk discounts for dorm essentials?

Yes, we do offer bulk discounts for dorm essentials! If you are purchasing items in large quantities, please contact our sales team directly through the 'Contact Us' page on our website. They will provide you with information on available discounts and help you with your order.

We aim to make your shopping experience as cost-effective as possible, especially for students preparing for dorm life. Don't hesitate to reach out for more details!
