THE BACKSTORY

Built by a  college parent. Approved by a college parent

We built MyCampusKorner to save families from the frantic, page-to-page panic of shopping for college move-in day.

As a former E-Commerce Operations Manager for a global corporation, I spent years building and managing large-scale digital commerce platforms. Yet when it came time to help our own sons prepare for their undergraduate education, my wife and I found ourselves sitting at the kitchen table experiencing the same stress that so many families face navigating countless websites to determine what they actually needed and where to find it.
