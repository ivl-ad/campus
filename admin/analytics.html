From Cloudflare Web Analytics: Cloudflare's own count of the same site, by a small script it adds to every page. No cookies either. Its visits are page views that came from another site or a direct link; page addresses are counted without their ?id=… part, so every product shows as /product (the Analytics tab has them one by one, plus the clicks to retailers). On busy days Cloudflare counts a sample and scales it up, so these are estimates. Ad blockers hide some visitors from both counts. Days are UTC days.

Counted by the site itself, without cookies: each page view adds 1 to that day's totals and nothing is kept about the person — no IP address, no identifier, no history across visits. A visit is a page view that did not come from another page of this site; “where visits come from” is the site that linked here (or the utm_source tag on the link). Clicks to retailers are visitors leaving through a Buy button, a partner logo or a link in a post. Counting started when this was deployed; days are Michigan days. Crawlers are left out, and so is any browser where “Don’t count this browser” is on.
